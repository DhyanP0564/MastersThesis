\subsection{The doubled space}
\label{app:doubled-space}
% LAND: the lifted linear terms D0 (x) (.), the folds n_1 and n_x, the tripled
%   space where a cubic term needs it; the measured kernel dimension (>= N^2 - 2N)
%   against the paper's >= 2^{2n-1} (claim A-10).

\Cref{sec:pihm-doubled} states the published construction as quadratic only. A term of degree $p$ in the unknown needs $p$ copies, $\ket{\Psi} = \ket{\psi}^{\otimes p}$, of dimension $N^p$, with its $p$ factors folded back into one Chebyshev series by $p-1$ applications of the product rule (a cubic term by a tripled space and two folds, a quartic by a quadrupled space and three). The register therefore grows with the degree, a limitation noted in the concurrent work of Paine \cite{paine2026concurrent}, and the argument of \Cref{prop:no-penalty} should carry over, since the kernel of the corresponding Hamiltonian is again a subspace and the $p$-fold product states are not; this thesis does not use it beyond $p=2$. The alternative it builds for higher degrees is the Carleman lift of \Cref{sec:carleman}.
